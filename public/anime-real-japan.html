Anime → Real Japan

You've seen it in Naruto. Here's the actual text it came from.

Japanese creators have always helped themselves from their own tradition. A mudra here, a fox spirit there, a dance that used to be performed for the kami. For a Japanese viewer much of this is background noise, something you grow up with. For everyone else it can be the first glimpse of a very old world.

In this section I take one series at a time and ask a simple question: what did the author actually use, and what does it mean in Japan? Sometimes the answer is surprisingly close to the source. Sometimes the anime version has very little left in common with it, and that's fine too, because it's fiction. Every article ends where the tradition still lives today, in temples, shrines and mountains you can actually visit.

One note so nobody gets the wrong idea: this site isn't connected to any of the series, studios or publishers. I'm a Japanologist who likes good stories and the real things behind them.
